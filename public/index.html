Our story

Warm homes, healthy puppies.

We raise every puppy with kindness in a calm, family-centered environment. Our litters are socialized around children, other animals and daily routines so they arrive ready for your home.

Here, every day feels like a happy playdate: warm collars, gentle cuddles, and plenty of tail wags. We love sharing puppy smiles with families who are ready to welcome a little furry friend.

Care first

Every puppy receives age-appropriate vaccinations, a microchip, and attentive socialization so they can thrive in your home.

Family focus

Our puppies grow up surrounded by love, gentle routines, and plenty of human attention so they are ready to become part of your family.

Meet the team

The team behind TIMBER CREEK

The TIMBER CREEK team cares for these puppies every day. Their love, patience and attention are the heart of every match.
